DigiWave Solutions Payment Manager
=================================

Version 1.1

Features
- Mobile-first Android client payment tracker
- Add, edit, reactivate, archive/former, or delete clients
- AED and PKR currencies
- Monthly fee and next due date
- WhatsApp number per client
- One-tap WhatsApp payment reminder with pre-filled message
- Partial payments and Paid in Full shortcut
- Automatic next-month due date after full payment
- Payment history per client
- Due Today / Due Soon / Overdue tracking
- Daily Android notifications for payments due within 3 days or overdue
- Custom reminder hour in Settings
- Search clients by name or WhatsApp number
- Active and Former client sections
- Offline local SQLite database, no login required
- Reminders restored after phone restart

Important
The WhatsApp button opens the client's chat with a pre-filled message. The user taps Send manually.

App name: DigiWave Payments
Package: com.digiwave.payments
Minimum Android: 8.0 (API 26)
Target SDK: 35
